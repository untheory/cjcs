UN Theory — Full Corpus Index

Welcome to the interactive primary catalog for UN Theory — The Unified Information Substrate, authored by Clint Jefferys. This repository organizes the formal mathematical derivations, non-perturbative RG fixed-point proofs, Standard Model gauge/spinor emerging structures, and multi-probe observational validation papers that constitute the full framework.

The corpus is systematically structured into four primary release tiers:

  • Core Theorem Papers (I–IV): Formal mathematical derivations establishing emergent geometry, scale invariance, su(3) ⊕ su(2) ⊕ u(1) Lie algebras, and global action convexity.
  • 12-Sector Multi-Probe Validation: Empirical constraints and quantitative proofs spanning observational cosmology, planetary dynamics, deep-Earth compression, and particle physics.
  • Sector & Machinery Monographs: Specialist treatises detailing microscopic correlation dynamics, parity-odd trispectra, and Hamiltonian operational machinery.
  • Restricted Structural Volumes: Extended axiomatic drafts, comprehensive equation indexes, and foundational correspondence layers held for peer-review and institutional access.
